The quiet return of the dumbphone

Sales of basic phones are up for the third year in a row. The people buying them aren’t nostalgic. They’re tired.

By Mara Lindqvist · November 12, 2026 · 7 min read

When Jonas Berg swapped his smartphone for a €49 flip phone last spring, he gave himself a week. Eight months later the flip phone is still in his pocket, and the smartphone sits in a drawer, charged once a month for banking.

He is part of a small but growing group. Retailers across Northern Europe say basic-phone sales rose 14 percent this year, the third increase in a row, and the fastest growth is among people under 30.

“It’s not really about the phone,” Berg says. “It’s about not carrying a feed around. I still use the internet. I just use it when I sit down to use it.”
